    Of the earth. From the hands of a human.

    Days to make. Millennia to master.

    Hand-faceted, micro-set and finished by master artisans in Thailand — the birthplace of stone-setting.

    Each jewel is made by hand, often over days — hand-faceted and micro-pavé set stone by stone under a microscope. Built on the five-millennia-old lost-wax artform, it’s kept alive by generational artisans in Thailand whose livelihood the house preserves. Tradition and disruptive modernity in one piece.

    Conscious materials

    Across every medium — semi-precious, recycled and premium man-made (AAA cubic zirconia) — on solid 925 silver, in 18K gold vermeil or premium black rhodium. In Warrior Soul, a natural black diamond is her signature, with a share of profit donated to repair the damage of irresponsible mining.

    “I want anyone who wears my jewellery to understand the creative intent, conscious dedication and loving hand workmanship in each piece.”— Sally Skoufis

    Artist. Founder. Global change agent.

    Original in a world of sameness.

    Sally Skoufis is not following the industry. She is challenging it.

    An Australian artist, designer and founder, Sally established SALLY SKOUFIS™ in 2012 to challenge convention and redefine what contemporary adornment could be. As the industry drifts toward sameness, she has stayed uncompromisingly original — edgy, modern and distinctive, grounded in artistic expression, modern asymmetry and her signature use of black. Each stone is sought out by hand and treated as a creative instrument. A pioneer of conscious materiality, she believes beautiful design should carry meaning — individuality over conformity, consciousness over excess, meaning over status.
